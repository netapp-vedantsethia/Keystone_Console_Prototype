  1. Right sizing — align commit and growth to real usage.

Workflow guidance

Key sizing expectations:

  1. Ask for starting used capacity, term, and growth rate; suggest minimum billing capacity with optional override.
  2. Use the term "Minimum Billing Capacity" consistently.
  3. Show alerts when sizing appears too high.
  4. Show NPV-based TCO for Keystone.
  5. Assume controller sizing is deferred to workload subscription-engine logic; ensure end-of-term forecast is supportable.
  6. Target outcome: user chooses capacity/workload and receives the mapped Keystone service and SLA.
